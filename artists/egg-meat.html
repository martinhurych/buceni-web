Egg Meat

Egg Meat is the horrible produce of Tooth Rust aka Laurel Uziell and Georgie McVicar, featuring the vocal folds resonating inside the throat of the poet Danny Hayward. The source of these vibrations does not mean any relation of identity can be drawn between the speaker, the sounds, and their segmentation into words. Maybe the words are spoken, but they do not speak: at times surfacing as meaningful utterances, at others buried under dense sonic matter.
