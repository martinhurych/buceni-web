Domizako

domizako is a Slovak DJ and producer based in Prague, resident of FUCHS2, co-founder of Motherspells FINTA, weaving global bass, up-tempo beats and deconstructed vocals into sets that are equal parts sexy — spots between tracks, not defined by genre but by vibe, energy and pure late-night chaos. As part of the Gravity Network roster 2024, domizako embraces artistic independence and creative freedom, continually pushing her sound into fresh territory while building a distinctive profile across local and regional stages. She has steadily grown her presence with a series of releases and performances that highlight her evolving style, appearing alongside notable acts and contributing to standout lineups such as Lunchmeat and Pohoda festivals.
